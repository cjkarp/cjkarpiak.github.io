About

CJ Karpiak

Writer and creative producer in Los Angeles, working from a theater background — which means I think in performance, pacing, and the quiet moments that land. That instinct runs through everything: the shot that earns emotion rather than demanding it, the line that never sounds like it came out of a conference room.

I write and produce most of what I make, and direct the projects where story, vision, and cast align. I’m at my best on sports work and anything built on real dialogue between characters worth watching — the kind of film people finish, then repeat.
