About

CJ Karpiak

A writer and creative producer (and on special occasions, director) whose Chicago theater roots left a deep appreciation for performance and an ear for pacing, forever chasing the thrill of creating memorable moments.

A quixotic devotion to Shakespeare belies my days spent in the exciting short-form worlds of Los Angeles-based studio work, branded content and modular assets.

(Long-form) narrative work coming soon.
